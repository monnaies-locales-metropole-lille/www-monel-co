# Paths opcache must never cache.
#
# opcache.validate_timestamps is 0 because the webroot is immutable for the life of
# the container. SPIP's compiled skeletons are the exception: they live under
# _DIR_TMP, they are generated at runtime, and they are expected to change. With
# timestamp validation off, opcache would serve a stale compiled template forever.
#
# Excluding them costs a little CPU on cache misses and buys correctness.
